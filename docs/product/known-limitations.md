# Known Limitations

- Spreadsheet upload and export, payments, and the About page are not built. The Express server, authentication, service requests, staff dashboard, owner admin, reviews, contact, moderation, migrations 001 through 017, and `pnpm seed` are in the repo. The About page waits on copy from Mason ([#24](https://github.com/masontuft/routemaster/issues/24)).
- Customers arrive by link. Staff create and revoke links on the customer page and copy them by hand; nothing sends them by email or text yet.
- Texting links through Sprinkler Messenger is half built. Routemaster can stage a batch, give a deep link, and serve it to the app through two token-authenticated JSON routes ([ADR 0015](../adr/0015-messenger-handoff-api.md)). The Messenger app does not yet open that link, send per-customer messages or report results, so a staged batch stays at "not yet reported". Opt-out and consent handling is not built (roadmap). Spec: [docs/integrations/sprinkler-messenger.md](../integrations/sprinkler-messenger.md) ([issue 37](https://github.com/masontuft/routemaster/issues/37)).
- Generic-link matching is exact after normalization; typos go to the Needs Review queue, where staff match them by hand.
- Matching a Needs Review request cannot edit the name, phone or street the visitor typed; a new customer is created exactly as typed plus the city and ZIP staff enter.
- Status changes have no undo: Completed and Cancelled are final.
- Login, magic-link redemption, the generic `/schedule` posts, and the contact form are rate limited in memory, per instance. The counts reset on restart.
- Single company only. Branded business workspaces are planned on the [roadmap](roadmap.md#branded-business-workspaces) and are not a commitment.
- Spreadsheet import from a public Google Sheet link is built: tab list, column mapping, preview, and a confirmed write. There is no `.xlsx` upload, no per-value status picker (one status applies to the whole import), and no undo ([#33](https://github.com/masontuft/routemaster/issues/33)). A row with no date adds a customer and property but no job. A row with no city (and no Default City) is still imported: its property has a null city, the preview and result page count them, and staff add the city afterward from the customer's property edit page, and the customer list can be filtered to customers missing one. Imported jobs record the sheet's amount in `amount_cents`, which no screen shows yet. The decided scope is jobs, customers and areas. Expenses, mileage and compressor tabs are outside that scope.
- Deleting customers is permanent: there is no soft delete and no undo, and a customer's properties, requests, reviews and links go with them. A batch delete removes the customers that import created. Jobs an import added to a customer who was already on file stay, and so does that customer. Requests with no customer (Needs Review) are not touched. Nothing records who deleted what.
- Area days are entered by hand. Nothing creates areas' days from the spreadsheet, so until staff add days a customer's date is only a preference. An area cannot be deleted, only renamed, because properties point at it. The public `/schedule` page does not use area days, since that would show whether a visitor is a customer. There is no limit on how many requests one day can take.
- Links for an area are made in bulk but not sent. The owner or dispatcher copies the table or CSV into a texting tool. The links appear only on the page that makes them, so leaving or refreshing it loses them and a rerun makes new ones. A batch holds at most 500 links. Only customers whose phone has at least 10 digits get a link. Nothing records which links were actually texted. Sending from the app, through Sprinkler Messenger or otherwise, stays on the [roadmap](roadmap.md).
- Customers cannot edit their own details; only staff can, and name and street are owner-only. There is no record of who changed what.
- Import matching uses the normalized street only, not the city, so two towns with a street of the same name can collide. The preview is mandatory for that reason; a street that belongs to a different customer is flagged, never merged. See [ADR 0009](../adr/0009-spreadsheet-import-and-sync.md).
- No two-way spreadsheet sync.
- Free Supabase projects may pause after inactivity. Resume the project before grading. See the [deployment runbook](../operations/deployment-runbook.md).
- Rebooking reads the customer's requests only. A customer served this year counts as booked, so a service done more than once a year, or a season that spans New Year, is not handled (both are on the [roadmap](roadmap.md)). The app records that a link was made, not that the customer answered a call or text. Links for all areas give a customer with several properties to the area of their first property, because properties have no type yet; a landlord may be linked under a rental's area.
- A penciled-in (tentative) booking sends nothing to the customer. They see it when they open their link, and a link made earlier may have expired. It needs an area that works the chosen day. If the owner turns the option off, existing tentative bookings stay but are left off routes.
- A free-text area column in an import creates one area per spelling, and imported properties may have no city. An area's city fills the gap for map lookups. There is no tool to merge or tidy areas.
- Technicians do not get logins for now. The dispatcher manages their routes. A technician role is planned on the [roadmap](roadmap.md#technician-logins) and is not a commitment.
- Referral payouts are handled outside the app. Staff record who referred the job on the request page.
- The seeded service types start at 0 cents. Until real prices are entered, the public services page says "Contact us for pricing." and owner money stats for those catalog prices read $0.00 ([#23](https://github.com/masontuft/routemaster/issues/23)).
- Replying to a contact message records a note and sets the status; nothing is emailed.
- Spam control is not built now. Reviews have no rate limit. Contact and the generic `/schedule` link have the in-memory per-IP limiter and CSRF. Three layers (a hidden honeypot, per-IP rate limiting, and Cloudflare Turnstile) are planned on the [roadmap](roadmap.md#spam-control) and are not a commitment.
- A review can only be written for a Completed request, so customers who never use the site cannot review.
- Routes are staff-only and estimated. `/staff/routes` orders a day's booked jobs with nearest-neighbor plus a 2-opt improvement, which is fast and usually close but not guaranteed to be the shortest. Distances are straight-line, so drive times are estimates (2 minutes per mile plus a 5-minute buffer, at least 5), and road closures, one-way streets and traffic are not considered. There is one route per day and one starting point, so two crews on the same day need a schema change. Technicians cannot see a route, and a route cannot be printed or exported yet.
- Map points come from Google (when `GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY` is set) and OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Without a key, each lookup waits about a second, and one click looks up at most 15 addresses. Properties store a city and ZIP but no state, so `SERVICE_STATE` is added to each lookup. An address with no city and no ZIP is not looked up. A job without a map point is left off the route and listed for staff to fix. The saved point is cleared when a street, city or ZIP changes, but not if the area or notes change.
- The day board books only requests that are still Requested, one status change each. A crash partway through leaves some booked; running it again skips the ones already booked. There is no per-day capacity limit, so a day can be over-booked.
