# Learn the lingo

Use AI to find out what you do not know, then reuse the terms in later prompts and in the architecture file.

- Ask: "What is that architectural pattern? What's the term?" Answers might be producer-consumer, observer, and so on.
- Ask for specs and links. Push back: "I don't believe you, show me the spec."
- Keep a new-term list (a "new term bucket"), and use those terms in later prompts and in architecture files.
- Off keyboard: talk through architecture concepts by voice (Gemini Live), for example monolith vs. microservices, so you can direct AI more precisely.

> "Even tell the AI, I don't believe you, you're lying to me. Show me the spec."
