# Modified Chicago case for UI copy

Use this for every heading from H1 to H6, plus titles, call-outs, and calls to action. AI tends toward title case on every heading, which looks ugly. This is strict Chicago casing with one modification for colons and semicolons. Mason still judges edge cases such as brand names and proper nouns.

1. Capitalize the first and last word of the title or subtitle.
2. Capitalize every major word: nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs.
3. Lowercase articles (a, an, the), coordinating conjunctions (and, but, for, nor, or), and all prepositions, whatever their length.
4. Capitalize both parts of a hyphenated compound.
5. Modification: Chicago lowercases the word after a colon. Do not. Treat colons and semicolons like a period and continue, so casing stays consistent across the site.

Examples:

- `Tips for Writing Code without Shortcuts`: "for" and "without" are prepositions, so they stay lowercase even though "without" is long.
- `Building a Mobile-First Layout: Why Small Screens Come First`: both parts of "Mobile-First" are capitalized, and "Why" is capitalized after the colon.

> "Treat colons, semicolons as a period, and just continue going."
