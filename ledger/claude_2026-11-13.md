## Fail fast when the database does not answer

- **Changed:** `src/db/pool.js` (default `connectionTimeoutMillis` of 10 seconds, exported as `CONNECTION_TIMEOUT_MS`, callers can override), `src/deploy.test.js` (two tests), this file. Branch `fix/pool-connection-timeout`, from `main` at 78df1d1. Uncommitted.
- **Why:** The Render deploy of 78df1d1 built, then timed out after 15 minutes with no start-command or app output. `pg.Pool` waits forever for a connection, so a database that accepts the socket and never answers would hang `pnpm migrate` and keep `pnpm start` from running. Cause of that deploy is not confirmed; this makes the next one fail in seconds with an error in the logs.
- **Decisions:** 10 seconds, a constant and not an environment variable (Mason chose 10). Chose a connection timeout, not `statement_timeout`, because a migration may run long. No retry loop, since it hides the failure. The timeout also bounds waiting for a free pool client when all 10 are busy.
- **Open / next:** A paused or cold Supabase project that takes over 10 seconds to answer now fails the deploy; a redeploy fixes it. Whether Supabase was paused on 2026-11-10 is unchecked. A keep-alive ping for the free plan's inactivity pause is discussed, not built. Verified: `pnpm lint` clean, `pnpm test` 499 of 499 against `TEST_DATABASE_URL`.
