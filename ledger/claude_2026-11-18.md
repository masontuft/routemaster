## Companies, step one: the table and the columns

- **Changed:** `migrations/028_create_companies.sql` (new), `src/db/migrate.test.js` (the applied list and four new tests), `docs/adr/0018-companies-and-company-scoped-data.md` (new, Proposed) and its row in `docs/adr/README.md`, a note in `docs/adr/0001-app-type-and-repo-shape.md`, the isolation TODO in `docs/product/roadmap.md` (now "Decided"), `docs/architecture/RouteMaster-Database-Schema.md` and `docs/architecture/database-erd.md` (regenerated with `pnpm erd`), `CHANGELOG.md`, this file. Branch `feat/companies-foundation`, in a worktree off `origin/main` (after PR #73).
- **Why:** Mason asked to start the branded workspaces work with separated business workspaces, and chose "a companies table with company_id on the shared tables" over a schema or tables per company. The unit is called company, not workspace.
- **Decisions:** (1) Migration 028 is additive only. `company_id` is `NOT NULL DEFAULT 1`, because the deploy runs `pnpm migrate` before the new code, and the old code's inserts do not set it. (2) No existing unique key is changed in 028, because `ON CONFLICT` in `appSetting.js`, `importBatch.js`, `area.js`, `import/apply.js` and `lead.js` names those constraints. The per-company keys and the `DROP DEFAULT` go in a later migration shipped with the model changes. (3) Twelve root tables get the column; the seven child tables are reached through their parent; `roles`, `request_statuses` and `service_types` stay shared. (4) Company 1 is "Tuft Sprinkler Blowouts" with slug `tuft-sprinkler-blowouts` (Mason named it in a follow-up on 2026-11-18; the slug is my derivation from the name, and nothing reads it yet). (5) Composite foreign keys on `(id, company_id)` are recorded in the ADR as open, not built; `SET NULL (lead_id)` needs Postgres 15 or later, and I did not check the Supabase version.
