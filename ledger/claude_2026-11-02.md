## Staff edit customers and properties

- **Changed:** `src/utils/permissions.js` and test, `src/utils/validation.js`, `src/utils/errors.js` (`forbidden`, moved out of `middleware/auth.js`), `src/models/customer.js` and `property.js` (update, add) and `models.test.js`, `src/controllers/staff.js`, `src/routes/staff.js`, `src/views/staff/customer-edit.ejs`, `property-edit.ejs`, `customer.ejs`, `customers.ejs`, `src/customerEdit.test.js`, endpoints, known limitations, `CHANGELOG.md`, this file.
- **Why:** Staff had no way to fix a customer's details or to fill in the city of an imported property that had none. Branch `feat/customer-edit`.
- **Decisions:** One map (`EDITABLE`) says which role edits which field and drives both the form and the route, so they cannot disagree. Name and street are owner-only because customer matching (scheduling form, import) reads them. A dispatcher edits phone, email, city, ZIP and notes. A posted field the role may not change is a 403 and nothing is saved. Normalized name, phone and address are recomputed on every edit. A property added by hand still requires a city; an edit may leave city or ZIP blank. The customer list can be filtered to customers with a property missing a city.
- **Open / next:** No audit trail of who changed what. Customers cannot edit their own details. Editing a name or street can leave two customers with the same normalized key, which matching then treats as one; the edit does not check for that. Verified on this branch: `pnpm lint` clean, `pnpm test` 289 of 289 against `TEST_DATABASE_URL`. No browser walkthrough.

## Owner deletes customers

- **Changed:** `src/models/customer.js` (`countRelated`, `remove`, `countClear`, `clear`), `src/models/importBatch.js` (`listWithRemaining`, `exists`), `src/controllers/staff.js` (delete page and post), new `src/controllers/clear.js`, `src/routes/staff.js` and `admin.js`, views `staff/customer-delete.ejs`, `admin/clear.ejs`, `admin/clear-confirm.ejs`, `staff/customer.ejs`, nav link in `partials/head.ejs`, tests `customerDelete.test.js` and `customerClear.test.js`, endpoints, known limitations, threat model, `CHANGELOG.md`, this file. Branch `feat/owner-delete-customers`.
- **Why:** Mason asked for the owner to be able to clear customers from the database: one at a time and in bulk.
- **Decisions:** Hard delete through the existing `ON DELETE CASCADE` keys, in one statement. A single delete is owner-only in the controller. Bulk delete is by import batch (the customers that batch created) or all customers behind the typed phrase `delete all customers`. The POST carries the counts the owner saw and `LOCK TABLE customers IN SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE MODE` is held while it recounts and deletes, so a mismatch gives 409 and deletes nothing. Jobs added by hand to an imported customer are deleted with them (Mason's default). Users, import batches and mappings, service types and areas are kept.
- **Open / next:** A batch delete does not undo what an import added to a customer who was already on file (their new property and jobs stay). No audit trail. Areas (`subdivisions`) left behind are not cleaned up. Whether the Supabase project has a restorable backup is not recorded in the repo; Mason should check before using delete-all on real data. No browser walkthrough.
