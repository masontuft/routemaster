## Put customer review of unusual names on the roadmap

- **Changed:** `docs/product/roadmap.md` (two new bullets, after "Property type": customer review of unusual names, and fixing a flagged name in the import preview), this file. Branch `docs/roadmap-customer-name-review`, from `main` at 20d28c5. Docs only; no code, schema or tests.
- **Why:** While planning the name normalization rules, Mason asked whether the customer could review unusual names too. The plan makes that a second, later change, and Mason asked for it to go on the roadmap.
- **Decisions:** Confirm-or-suggest, not free editing: a link is a bearer token, so a correction waits for staff approval. Staff review at import stays. Name decisions agreed: a joint name stays one customer and is handled automatically, and Jr/Sr stay in the match key. Fixing in the preview went on the roadmap as an option: sheet rows have no stable id, so a fix would need a saved list matched to rows another way. Corrected the first bullet, which listed joint names as flagged.
